# symfony-accounting-be

Version: 6.0.5

Belgian law for symfony-accounting: PCMN chart, enterprise numbers, structured communications, CODA bank files, periodic VAT return, client and intra-EU listings, legal mentions

## Table of Contents

- [Introduction](#introduction)

`symfony-accounting-be` brings Belgian law to `symfony-accounting`: the PCMN, enterprise and VAT numbers, structured communications printed on invoices and matched in bank files, CODA bank statements, the periodic VAT return by grid, the annual client listing and the intra-Community listing, and the invoice mentions of the VAT code and of the law of 2 August 2002.

A ledger whose country is `BE` uses it as soon as the bundle is registered. Legal texts and the PCMN data must be confirmed by an accountant before use.
